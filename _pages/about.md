👋 Hi, I'm Ahmed, a Mechanical Engineering master's student at McMaster University, Canada. I am doing my research at the McMaster Automotive Research Centre (MARC), in the field of EV battery thermal management and physics-informed AI/ML. 🔋🚗

🎓 I graduated from Alexandria University, Egypt, with excellence, ranking in the top 2% of my class. I've worked as a Mechanical Design Engineer in the field of water solutions and desalination, and I hold a Teacher Assistant position in the department of the Engineering Mathematics and Physics, Alexandria University, Egypt. 👨‍🏫

🔬 I am currently building a 12-cell battery module test rig with liquid cold-plate cooling, to study the thermal behavior of the cells under high C-rate charging, discharging, and drive cycles [The Project](https://ahmedalkadi.github.io/Ahmed_Alkadi_Portfolio.github.io/portfolio/battery-module-test-rig/).

📚 I have publications in the fields of energy storage and battery thermal management: a journal paper on phase change material thermal storage [The Paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352152X23002293?via%3Dihub), and a conference paper on an AI agent-based framework for battery thermal management, published at IEEE ITEC 2026 [The Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITECEATS66641.2026.11592979). I have also contributed to designing cooling systems for EV batteries. 🚗💡

I’m passionate about Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), Finite Element Analysis (FEA), renewable energy, and machine learning. 🌱🔧

✈️ I'm an airplane enthusiast and honed my practical skills through my participation as a Mechanical Engineer with the [Lycans Team](https://www.facebook.com/Lycans.Aircraft) for over three seasons in the SAE competition in the USA.

🎓 I was awarded a fully funded scholarship to study machine learning in a professional manner at the Information Technology Institute (ITI), Egypt, providing a comprehensive understanding of AI and its applications. This includes concepts like supervised/unsupervised learning, neural networks, deep learning, and natural language processing.

🤖 I enjoy tackling traditional engineering challenges with the power of AI, blending technology and innovation to find smarter solutions [GitHub pages](https://github.com/ahmedalkadi).
